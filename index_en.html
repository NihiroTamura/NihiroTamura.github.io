WelcomeNIHIRO TAMURA  /  OSAKA UNIVERSITY

I am Nihiro Tamura, a Ph.D. student at the Ishiguro Laboratory (Intelligent Robotics Laboratory), Department of Systems Innovation, Graduate School of Engineering Science, Osaka University. I study androids — robots designed to closely resemble human beings — focusing on the "human-likeness."

I believe that whether people can accept androids as human beings depends on their “human-likeness.” The more human-like an android looks, the more we expect it to move like a human and interact with us naturally, as another person would. If its movements appear unnatural or its interactions feel unlike those between people, it becomes difficult for us to accept the android as a human being.

My current interest is in designing human-likeness from two perspectives: the android’s movements themselves and the interactions between the android and humans.
