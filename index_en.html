WelcomeNIHIRO TAMURA  /  OSAKA UNIVERSITY

I am Nihiro Tamura, a first-year doctoral student at the Ishiguro Laboratory (Intelligent Robotics Laboratory), Department of Systems Innovation, Graduate School of Engineering Science, Osaka University. I study androids — robots built to closely resemble human beings — focusing on the human-likeness of their motion and on how people give them instructions.

Maai is the Japanese word for the distance and timing held between two people. We close it and open it as the other person moves, and when that adjustment goes smoothly we feel the exchange is going well. Whether an android can be accepted as a human presence comes down to the same thing. The closer its appearance gets to ours, the more we expect it to keep a human maai: any deviation in its movement reads as strangeness, and any procedure demanded of us before it will move turns it back into a tool.

The motion an android produces, and the way people behave toward it — I want to design the maai from both sides.
